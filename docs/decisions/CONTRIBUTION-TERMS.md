# Contribution terms decision

**Status:** Approved 2026-08-09 for preparation
**Model:** DCO-based open contributions under MIT

Use Developer Certificate of Origin sign-off when contributions are accepted under
the project's MIT license. Contributors retain copyright in their contributions;
public issues, forks, and pull requests may be enabled only with the authorized
release.

No CLA or copyright assignment is required for V1. Do not add either merely for
hypothetical relicensing.

This approval prepares policy only. External contribution channels remain inactive
while the repository is private, and no sign-off text changes `LICENSE`, publishes
the repository, or sets `publicationAuthorized` to true.
