# Fuzit community conduct

Fuzit aims for a respectful, technically rigorous, harassment-free collaboration
space. Participants must be considerate, accept evidence-based review, respect
privacy and confidentiality, and avoid discriminatory, threatening, sexualized,
or personally identifying content.

Do not publish another person's identity, repository content, security report, or
private communication without authorization. Good-faith disagreement about code is
welcome; personal attacks, intimidation, repeated disruption, and retaliation are not.

Report conduct concerns privately through an owner-approved channel. Do not place
sensitive reports in public issues, commits, logs, or screenshots. The repository
owner may warn, restrict, reject, or remove participation proportionately while
minimizing disclosure. This policy creates no public reporting endpoint while the
project remains private.
